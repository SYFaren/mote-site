Welcome to mote!

Ultra-compact GUI text editors for Linux and Windows. Classic mote is squeezed hard; mote-x adds syntax, themes, wrap, multi-doc and more — still tiny.

Two sizes

mote mote-x
Original. Ultra-compact. Fixed set.
  • Find, goto, dup/del line
  • Bracket jump, auto-indent
  • ~17–33 KB on Linux
 Extended. Soft budget ~100–200 KB.
  • Syntax highlight, themes
  • Wrap, multi-doc, zoom
  • Replace, recent, EOL
